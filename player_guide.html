Neutral Zone

The Ultimate Strategic Territory Domination Game

Gameplay in action: Expanding territory and managing units.

Objective

Welcome Commander. Your objective in Neutral Zone is simple: eradicate the enemy or dominate the sector.

  • Domination: Expand your territory polygon by positioning Stations to capture 55% of the sector area.
  • Destruction: Destroy the enemy's Home Planet using Fighters and offensive pushes.
  • Bankruptcy: Attrite the enemy until they have 0 units and less than 25 Energy remaining.

The Fleet

You have three types of units at your disposal. Build them using Energy (E) from the left/right UI panels.

Miner (25 Energy, 5s Build)

Automatically searches for Asteroids located within your territory. Mines resources (up to 25 payload) and returns them to the Home Planet. Highly vulnerable with 60 HP.

Station (50 Energy, 10s Build)

The cornerstone of your expansion. Drag them across the map to anchor territory polygons. Automatically links with up to 2 nearby nodes within 5 grid units and fires defensive turrets at approaching enemy Fighters.

Fighter (100 Energy, 15s Build)

Your offensive muscle. Click or drag-select fighters, then drag to draw a custom waypoint flight path. They actively engage enemy fighters, stations, and the enemy Home Planet.

Taking action on the field. You can play as Human vs CPU, or CPU vs CPU to test strategies!

Mechanics & Strategy

Mastering Neutral Zone involves managing both economy, perimeter power, and space.

  • Territory Network: Territory is formed by planar graph polygons. Each Station links to its closest neighbors within 5 grid units. When 3 or more connected nodes form a closed loop, they project territory. Keep stations linked to maintain power!
  • Border Power: Each station expands your border perimeter capacity. Overextended borders turn red and limit further expansion.
  • Territory Advantage: Units move 50% slower when operating outside friendly territory. Expanding forward creates a tactical highway for your fleet.
  • Energy Pipeline: Without Miners, you will quickly starve of Energy. Protect your Asteroids! If an Asteroid falls outside your territory, miners will abort and retreat home.
  • Drawing & Clearing Paths: Select Fighters individually or with a drag box, then draw waypoint paths to flank enemies. Press Escape or X to clear flight paths.
